Privacy Policy

A clear shopping list, one item at a time.

Who is responsible

Target Bag is developed by Eren Avar. For privacy questions or requests, contact erenavar@gmail.com.

Shopping lists and settings

Item names, categories, completion status, language preferences, and purchase verification data are stored on your device. Target Bag has no app account and does not send your shopping list to a developer-operated server. Your operating system may include app data in its backups according to your device settings.

Store purchases

Apple or Google processes in-app payments and keeps the store transaction record under its own privacy policy. The app uses store product and transaction information to verify and restore the unlimited feature. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase verification information may be cached on your device.

App updates

Target Bag uses Expo’s EAS Update service to download app improvements. Update requests can include technical information such as your IP address, app/platform version, and installation information. This service is separate from your shopping list; list contents are not included in update requests.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request, and retain it while needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete information you sent.

Your choices and retention

Delete individual items or all items within the app. Removing the app or clearing its data removes its local data, subject to operating-system backup and restoration settings. Store purchase records are managed by Apple or Google and are separate from your list. You can request access, correction, or deletion of support information by email.

Advertising and children

Target Bag contains no advertising SDK or developer-added advertising tracking. We do not sell shopping-list or support data. The app is a general shopping-list tool and is not specifically directed at children. Please contact us if a child has sent personal information through support.

Services and this website

Service providers apply their own policies: Apple, Google, Expo, and GitHub. This static support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process request and IP information. We add no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or contact form to these pages.

Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change. The date above identifies the current policy.
