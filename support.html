Support

A clear shopping list, one item at a time.

Contact

For help with Target Bag, email erenavar@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS or Android, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or a full purchase receipt.

Free and unlimited items

The app is free to download. You can keep up to two items at a time across all categories. Items marked as purchased still count. Deleting an item frees a slot. A one-time purchase removes the item limit; there is no subscription. The US base price is US$0.99. Your store shows the price and currency for your region.

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account or Google Play account that made the purchase. In Target Bag, open Menu, then Add unlimited items, then Restore purchases. Connect to the internet. Purchases are restored within the store where they were made; an Apple purchase does not unlock the Android app, and a Google Play purchase does not unlock the iOS app.

Payments and refunds

Payments are processed by the App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. If a payment is pending, wait for the store to complete it. Cancelled or failed payments do not unlock unlimited items. For refund requests, use the store’s purchase support.

Lists and device changes

Shopping lists are stored on your device. Target Bag does not provide a list sync or cloud backup service. Device backup settings may affect whether data is restored. Restoring a purchase restores the unlimited feature; it does not restore your list.
